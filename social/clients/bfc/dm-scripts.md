# Boss & Friends: comment-to-DM scripts

This is the manual (free) version of a comment-to-DM funnel. Twice a day (12:30pm and 8:00pm PT), open **Meta Business Suite → Inbox** (covers FB and IG), then **TikTok → Inbox** and **LinkedIn → Notifications**, and work through the new comments.

**Rules**
- Reply publicly **and** DM, both within the same window. The public reply shows everyone else that the freebie is real. Replying quickly in the first hour also helps reach.
- Vary the public reply. Pasting the exact same line 30 times looks like a bot and can get flagged.
- Never ask for their phone or email. The whole point is "no email, no sign-up".
- Someone asks for a quote in the comments or DMs? Reply: "Send us a DM with the area and rough scope and we'll get back to you." Then pass it to the boss.
- Save each DM below as a **Saved Reply** in Meta Business Suite (Inbox → ⋯ → Saved replies) so it's two taps.

Links: paste the Drive "anyone with the link" URL for each PDF where it says `{LINK}`. They're recorded in `brand.json` → `freebies.<KEY>.link`.

## Platform notes
- **Instagram:** needs a Business or Creator account. If a DM lands in someone's *Requests* folder, the public reply ("check your message requests") covers it.
- **Facebook:** in Meta Business Suite, open the comment and use **Send private message**. That sends one private reply to the commenter.
- **LinkedIn:** a company Page can't DM from comments easily. Reply to the comment with the link directly (links in LinkedIn comments are clickable). This is the B2B audience, so a direct link is fine.
- **TikTok:** reply to the comment "Sent! Check your DMs". DMs only reach people who allow messages. If it fails, reply with "Link's in our bio" and put the current freebie link in the bio for that week.

## Later: automate it
Once this is running and people are clearly commenting keywords, move the POUR/HIRE/SAFE replies to a keyword automation (ManyChat has a free tier; check its current limits first). Keep these texts; they become the automation's messages.
